For many of us, the pandemic was a hard time. It felt like something was taken away from humanity — something we haven't quite gotten back yet. For me, one way of trying to process it was to make music. I started making songs every day. Therapy songs. Some are funny. Some are sad. Some are hopeful. Some are angry. Some are weird. Some are probably just bad. 😸 But I made them anyway. This album is basically what came out of that process. It's not really meant to be listened to in any particular order. Maybe one song will make you laugh. Maybe another one will hit you differently. Or maybe you'll just find something in here that makes you feel a little less alone. WARMLINES One thing that helped me a lot while I was making these songs was talking to people on warmlines. If you've never heard of them, they're peer-support phone lines where you can talk with someone when you need to talk — even if you're not in an immediate crisis. Many are free and confidential, and they're often staffed by people with their own lived experience. Mental Health America +1 I didn't know they existed for a long time. I'm really glad I found them. Maybe they'll help you, too. Find a Warmline⁠
